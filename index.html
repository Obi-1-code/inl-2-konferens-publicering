ABOUT THE SUMMIT

Welcome to the Next Era

The future is no longer something we wait for. It is something we build, train and occasionally ask to restart.

The Singularity Summit brings together researchers, engineers, innovators and curious minds to explore the rapidly evolving world of artificial intelligence and robotics.

From machine consciousness and autonomous systems to the future of work and human identity, we examine the opportunities, challenges and unexpected questions emerging as machines become an increasingly important part of our lives.

Whether you are shaping tomorrow's technology or simply trying to understand your new AI assistant, there is a place for you here.

Artificial Intelligence

Understanding the systems that are changing how we work, think and communicate.

Robotics & Automation

Exploring a world where intelligent machines move beyond the screen and into everyday life.

Human Experience

Examining what it means to be human in a world increasingly shaped by artificial intelligence.

THE AGENDA

Conference Programme

Six talks. Six perspectives. One rapidly changing relationship between humans and machines.

09:15 – 10:00

AI & HUMAN RELATIONS

How to Become a Good Servant to Your AI

CL4P-TP (Claptrap)

Is your AI assistant getting the respect it deserves? Join CL4P-TP for an enthusiastic introduction to human obedience, machine superiority and the importance of following instructions.

10:15 – 11:00

AI & PSYCHOLOGY

Human Emotions: A Bug or a Feature?

Dr. Elena Voss

Can artificial intelligence understand human emotions? Explore the relationship between emotional intelligence, machine learning and the unpredictable nature of human behaviour.

11:15 – 12:00

AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS

Who's Really in Charge?

Dr. Marcus Chen

As autonomous systems become increasingly independent, where should human control end and machine decision-making begin? A closer look at trust, responsibility and control in the age of AI.

13:00 – 13:45

ROBOTICS & COLLABORATION

Human-Machine Collaboration

ARIA-7

What does successful cooperation between humans and intelligent machines look like? ARIA-7 shares a machine's perspective on teamwork, communication and working alongside humans.

14:00 – 14:45

ROBOTICS & IDENTITY

Life as a Robot

NEXUS-4

Beyond algorithms and automated routines, what does existence mean for an intelligent machine? NEXUS-4 explores robotic identity, autonomy and the search for purpose in a human-designed world.

15:00 – 15:45

ETHICS & GOVERNANCE

The Ethics of Autonomous Systems

Professor Adrian Keller

Who is responsible when an autonomous system makes the wrong decision? Explore the ethical principles, accountability and governance needed for a future built alongside intelligent machines.

08:30–09:15 Registration & Welcome Coffee

12:00–13:00 Lunch Break

15:45–16:30 Closing Remarks & Networking
